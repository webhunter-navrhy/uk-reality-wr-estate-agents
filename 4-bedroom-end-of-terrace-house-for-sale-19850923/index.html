Description

Positioned within easy reach of Worcester City Centre, this spacious and characterful four-bedroom end-of-terrace home offers generous accommodation set across three floors, perfectly suited to growing families, those working from home or buyers seeking flexible living space. Combining period features with practical modern living, the property also benefits from a fantastic garden room, providing valuable additional space separate from the main house.

A welcoming porch opens into the entrance hallway, setting the tone for the accommodation beyond. To the front of the property, the attractive bay-fronted living room is filled with natural light, creating a comfortable and inviting space to relax. The separate dining room offers an ideal setting for family meals and entertaining, flowing effortlessly through an attractive archway into the kitchen to create a sociable layout while retaining clearly defined living spaces.

To the rear, a practical hallway provides access to the enclosed rear garden and leads through to the ground floor bathroom, offering a functional layout for everyday family life.

The first floor comprises three well-proportioned bedrooms, providing flexible accommodation for children, guests or home working. Occupying the entire top floor, the impressive loft conversion creates a generous fourth double bedroom, offering a peaceful retreat with excellent versatility as a principal suite, guest bedroom or additional living space.

Outside, the rear garden is mainly laid to lawn, complemented by an attractive seating area that provides the perfect setting for outdoor dining, entertaining or relaxing during the warmer months. At the top of the garden sits a superb garden room complete with power and lighting. This versatile space lends itself to a variety of uses, whether as a home office, gym, workshop, creative studio, games room or even a stylish garden bar for hosting family and friends.

Conveniently located within walking distance of Worcester City Centre, the property enjoys excellent access to a wide range of shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, leisure facilities and both railway stations, making it an ideal choice for commuters and families alike.

Offering spacious accommodation, versatile living spaces and a fantastic garden room, this impressive home presents a wonderful opportunity to enjoy flexible family living in a highly convenient location.
